Privacy Policy — Gallery - Telegram Gallery

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Gallery - Telegram Gallery is published by Divyansh007, operated by Divyansh Gupta. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact developerg675@gmail.com.

Gallery is an independent application and is not affiliated with Telegram or Google.

1. Your device library

With your Android permission, Gallery accesses photos, videos and associated information, such as filenames, dates and album names, to display and organise your library.

Gallery stores a local database containing its library index, settings and transfer information. Thumbnails, downloaded media and imported files may also be stored on your device. Granting media access does not automatically upload your library.

2. Telegram sign-in

Gallery uses your Telegram account rather than creating a separate developer-hosted Gallery account.

Information required for authentication—including your phone number, verification codes, name, optional verification email and Telegram password—is processed through Telegram’s client library. Telegram also receives connection and client information necessary to operate its service.

Account identifiers, session information and authentication keys are stored locally to maintain your connection. Sensitive settings and the Telegram database encryption key use Android Keystore-backed protection.

Gallery does not request access to your contacts or SMS inbox. Where available, Google Play services provides an optional system phone-number chooser.

3. Sync and automatic backup

Media is uploaded when you choose a sync, copy or move action, or enable automatic backup for selected albums.

Automatic backup is optional and may operate in the background using Wi-Fi or mobile data. You can disable it through the app’s settings.

Selected media and associated captions or metadata are transferred to your chosen Telegram destinations. Telegram controls its cloud storage, processing, availability and retention under its Privacy Policy. People with access to a destination channel may also access its contents.

You retain ownership of your media.

4. Connected channels

Gallery uses a channel helper bot to identify connected Telegram channels. It reads accessible channel history through your signed-in Telegram account. Folder sync can create or reuse eligible private channels and add the helper with the permissions required for that connection.

A Cloudflare-hosted integration processes Telegram updates associated with the helper. Depending on Telegram permissions and configuration, these updates can include channel identifiers, names, membership changes, channel posts, captions and media identifiers.

The integration does not forward new channel posts or media to the developer’s Telegram account.

5. App lock and Secure Folder

App lock and Secure Folder restrict access within Gallery. They do not remove or protect originals displayed in Telegram or other phone gallery applications.

Fingerprint authentication uses Android’s biometric system. Gallery does not receive fingerprint images or biometric templates.

6. Feedback and optional contributions

Feedback opens an editable email draft. Media and diagnostic logs are not automatically attached. If you send the email, the developer receives your email address and the information you include.

Optional contributions are processed by the payment application you choose. Gallery does not receive your banking PIN or payment credentials. The payment provider and recipient may receive transaction details through their payment services.

Gallery does not include advertising or developer analytics SDKs.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until removed through available app controls, Android’s Clear storage function or uninstallation, depending on the data type. Export any files stored only inside Gallery before clearing its storage.

Signing out disconnects the selected Telegram session; it does not delete your Telegram account, channels or phone originals.

Removing a channel from Gallery hides it without deleting Telegram content. Confirmed deletion actions can remove phone files and Telegram media. Permanently deleting an associated folder can delete its entire Telegram channel after confirmation. Android may request approval before deleting phone originals.

To delete your Telegram account, use Telegram’s account deletion service. This affects your Telegram account beyond Gallery.

Feedback correspondence is retained as needed to handle the conversation or request. You can request deletion of information held by the developer by emailing the privacy contact above.

8. Copies received under earlier versions

Earlier versions supported forwarding channel posts to the developer’s Telegram account. Disabling forwarding does not automatically delete copies already received.

To request deletion of those copies, email developerg675@gmail.com with the subject “Gallery data deletion request”. Identify the relevant account, channel and posts or date range. Do not send passwords, verification codes or unnecessary original media.

Deleting an original, uninstalling Gallery or deleting your Telegram account does not automatically remove copies held in another account.

9. Service providers

Relevant services operate under their own privacy policies:

- Telegram: account authentication and Telegram content.

- Google: applicable Android and Google Play services features.

- Cloudflare: hosting and operation of the channel integration.

These providers may process information in countries different from yours.

10. Eligibility and policy changes

Gallery is not directed to children. Telegram’s applicable minimum-age requirements also apply.

This policy may be updated when the app’s data handling changes. Material changes will be communicated, and consent will be requested where required before new processing begins.
