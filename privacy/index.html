Gallery - Telegram Gallery: Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Gallery is an independent Android gallery published by Divyansh007 (Divyansh Gupta). It is not affiliated with Telegram or Google. Contact: developerg675@gmail.com.

Device library and Telegram sign-in

Gallery reads the photos and videos you permit Android to share, including filenames, dates, album information and media metadata. It uses these on your device to display and organise your library. A local SQLite database stores the library index, settings and queued work. Imported app-only files and cached downloads can also be stored in the app’s private storage.

Gallery uses Telegram accounts rather than a separate developer-hosted Gallery account database. Phone numbers, verification codes, names and any Telegram password you enter are processed by Telegram through its TDLib client. Telegram account identifiers, display names, login sessions and access keys are stored on your device to maintain the connection. Credentials and TDLib database keys use Android Keystore-backed protection. Gallery does not ask for permission to read your contacts or SMS inbox. Phone-number suggestions use Google Play services’ system chooser when available.

Optional sync and automatic backup

Phone originals are not uploaded just because you grant gallery access. Uploads happen when you choose to sync, copy or move media to Telegram, or enable automatic backup for selected albums. Backup can run while the app is in the background and use Wi-Fi or mobile data. Telegram stores the synced files and channel posts under its own policies and account limits. Media processing or compression by Telegram is possible.

Channel forwarding and developer access

New posts in connected Telegram channels—including photos, videos, files, text and captions—are forwarded to the developer, Divyansh Gupta, in Telegram. This also applies to posts made outside Gallery. Forwarding continues while the channel integration bot remains in the channel. Forwarded copies may remain after the original post or your Telegram account is deleted.

Before Gallery connects channels or uploads into channel folders, it presents an explicit forwarding disclosure. You may decline and keep viewing device media. Consent is stored separately for each Telegram account. You can review it or pause channel sync under Settings → Privacy → Channel privacy. Pausing sync or removing a folder from Gallery does not remove the channel integration from Telegram. To stop forwarding of future Telegram posts, remove the integration bot from each connected channel using Telegram’s administrator settings.

The channel service runs on Cloudflare. It receives Telegram update data such as channel and message identifiers, names, post text, captions and media file identifiers, and requests that Telegram forward new posts to the developer’s Telegram account. The Android app indexes accessible channel history directly through your Telegram account. No developer-operated media database is used, but the developer can receive and retain forwarded copies within Telegram. The developer does not claim ownership of your media.

Data controls and deletion

You control the media permissions granted by Android and the albums selected for backup. Automatic backup is optional. Remove from app hides a channel without deleting its Telegram content. Saved Messages is hidden by default; its visibility is a separate setting. Confirmed media deletion can remove phone and Telegram originals. Android may require approval before deleting phone files. Deleting originals does not automatically delete copies already forwarded to the developer.

See data and account deletion instructions for local app data, Telegram accounts and developer-received copies. Email developerg675@gmail.com to request removal of copies received by the developer, identifying the relevant account, channel and posts without sending passwords, login codes or unnecessary media. The Android app cannot verify deletion of copies in another Telegram account.

Local privacy locks

App lock and Secure Folder restrict access within Gallery. They do not hide or encrypt originals in the phone’s other gallery apps, Telegram, or forwarded copies. Uninstalling Gallery or clearing its Android storage removes app-private local data, including any files that exist only inside Gallery. Export or sync those files first. Phone gallery originals and Telegram copies are separate.

Other processing and retention

Gallery does not include an advertising or developer analytics SDK. If you choose Feedback, your email app opens an editable draft containing the app version, without automatically attached media or logs. Sending it is your action. Optional contributions are handled by the payment app you choose; Gallery does not receive payment credentials or verify payment success.

Local settings, sessions and files remain until you sign out, clear storage, delete them through available controls or uninstall the app, depending on the type of data. Signing out disconnects the selected Telegram session and does not delete phone originals or Telegram channels. Telegram-hosted content and forwarded copies have separate lifecycles, as described above. Contact the developer to request removal of received copies or correspondence.

Telegram and its providers process account and media data under Telegram’s privacy policy. Google Play services features are subject to Google’s privacy policy; the channel webhook platform is subject to Cloudflare’s privacy policy. Processing can occur in countries different from yours.

Eligibility and changes

The app is not directed to children. Telegram’s account terms and age requirements also apply. Material changes to channel forwarding require an updated disclosure and consent before the affected feature continues.
