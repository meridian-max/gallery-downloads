Gallery - Telegram Gallery: Data and account deletion

Publisher: Divyansh007 (Divyansh Gupta). Last updated: 10 November 2026.

Gallery uses your Telegram account and stores its own library index and settings on your device. There is no separate developer-hosted Gallery account database. Local app data, Telegram content and developer-received copies must be handled separately.

Remove local Gallery data

  1. First export or finish syncing files that exist only inside Gallery.
  2. On Android, open Settings → Apps → Gallery - Telegram Gallery → Storage → Clear storage (wording varies by phone). You can reach this screen from Gallery → Settings → Privacy → Data & privacy → Open Android app settings.
  3. Confirm Android’s clearing action yourself. It removes Gallery’s local settings, index, login sessions, app-private downloads, local-only files and locks for all accounts in that installation.

Phone gallery originals and Telegram copies are kept. Uninstalling the app also removes its app-private local data. To disconnect only one Telegram session, use Gallery → Settings → Accounts → Sign out.

Delete Telegram media or your Telegram account

Use Gallery’s confirmed media/folder deletion controls or Telegram itself to remove the corresponding cloud content. Deleting a connected main folder can delete its entire associated channel after confirmation. To permanently delete the Telegram account itself, visit Telegram’s account deletion page, sign in directly with Telegram and complete its confirmation. This affects your Telegram account beyond Gallery. Telegram controls that process and its own retention rules.

Stop future channel forwarding

New posts from connected channels can be forwarded to the developer after your explicit channel consent. To stop this from continuing, remove the channel integration bot from every affected channel using Telegram’s administrator settings. Pausing sync or hiding/removing a channel inside Gallery does not remove that integration from Telegram.

Request deletion of copies received by the developer

Deleting an original, clearing Gallery’s storage, or deleting your Telegram account does not automatically remove copies already forwarded to the developer’s Telegram account. To request their removal, email developerg675@gmail.com with the subject “Gallery data deletion request”. Identify the Telegram account you own and the relevant channel, posts or date range so the request can be verified and the copies located. Do not send Telegram passwords, login codes or unnecessary original photos/videos.

The request can be sent without reinstalling Gallery. The app itself cannot verify whether another Telegram account has removed its copies. Telegram’s independent platform retention rules continue to apply.
